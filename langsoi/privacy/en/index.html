Privacy Policy — Wolf Village

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Wolf Village (Làng Sói) is an offline party-game host by Thinkling Studio. This page explains exactly what the app does, and does not do, with your information.

Summary

  • We collect nothing. No personal data, analytics, telemetry, crash reports or advertising identifiers.
  • No accounts, no internet, no server. We run no server of our own; games never leave the room.
  • No ads, no tracking. Wolf Village Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple Account.

Player names and game history

Names you type for a table, your settings and the history of finished games are stored only on your device. You can clear the history in the app at any time.

Playing on several devices (local network)

When you host or join a table on your own devices, the app uses the local network (Wi-Fi) to connect phones in the same room directly. Only what the game needs is exchanged — player names, seats, the secret role sent to each player, and night actions and votes — and the connection is encrypted with a key shared through the table's QR code or six-letter code. Nothing is sent over the internet. A small rejoin token is kept in the device Keychain so you can return to a table after a drop.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan a table's QR code. Frames are processed on the device; no photo or video is saved or transmitted. You can type the six-letter code instead.

Third parties

No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

Wolf Village collects no personal data from anyone, including children, and has no online chat or content shared outside the room.

Deleting your data

All data lives on your device; deleting the app deletes it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
