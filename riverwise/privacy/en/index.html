Privacy Policy — Riverwise

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Riverwise: Learn Xiangqi is private by default. This page explains exactly what the app does, and does not do, with your information.

Summary

  • We collect nothing. No personal data, analytics, telemetry, crash reports or advertising identifiers.
  • Everything stays on your device. Lessons, puzzle and chapter progress, games, coins, cosmetics, settings and your profile name and avatar are stored locally.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Riverwise Plus (subscription or one-time purchase) is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple Account; the app only asks StoreKit on your device whether Plus is active.
  • We run no server of our own.

On-device processing

The xiangqi engine, hints, move reviews and the coach explanations run entirely on your device. No position or text data is sent anywhere.

Optional same-Wi-Fi play (local network)

Two devices on the same Wi-Fi network can play each other directly using Apple's Network framework with Bonjour (_riverwise._tcp) and an encrypted connection protected by a 4-digit code. Only moves, game controls (draw, takeback, resign, rematch) and each player's chosen profile name and avatar are exchanged between the two devices. Nothing goes to an external server, and there is no chat. iOS asks for Local Network permission the first time you host or join.

Permissions

Local network — only for the optional same-Wi-Fi game. The app requests no other permissions (no location, contacts, camera, microphone or tracking).

Third parties

No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Bundled third-party assets are listed in Settings → About → Licenses.

Children's privacy

Riverwise collects no personal data from anyone and has no chat or user-generated content shared online.

Deleting your data

All data lives on your device; deleting the app deletes it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
