Riverwise — Support

Riverwise: Learn Xiangqi (Riverwise – Học Cờ Tướng) by Thinkling Studio.

Get help

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue at github.com/vietvk92/vietvk92.github.io/issues. Please include your device model and iOS version.

Common questions

  • Restore purchases: open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Riverwise Plus is tied to your Apple Account.
  • Cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Riverwise.
  • Same-Wi-Fi game will not connect: both devices must be on the same network and allow Local Network access (iPhone Settings → Riverwise). Some public Wi-Fi blocks devices from seeing each other; use a personal hotspot instead.
  • My progress: everything is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes progress.

Hỗ trợ (Tiếng Việt)

Gửi câu hỏi hoặc báo lỗi tại github.com/vietvk92/vietvk92.github.io/issues. Khôi phục giao dịch: Cài đặt trong ứng dụng → Khôi phục giao dịch.
