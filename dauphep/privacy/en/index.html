Privacy Policy — Wand Clash (Đấu Phép)

Updated: 21 October 2026

Wand Clash is a spell-duel game by Thinkling Studio: two players hold iPhones like wands and draw spells in the air. This page explains what the app does, and does not do, with your information.

Summary

  • We collect nothing. No personal data, no analytics, no automatic crash reports, no advertising identifiers.
  • No account, no internet, no server. Every duel runs directly between nearby devices.
  • No ads, no tracking. “Unlock All” is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple Account.

Motion sensors

The app reads the phone's motion sensors to recognise the spell you draw in the air, and to pause the match if the phone is dropped. This data is processed on the device, never stored and never sent.

Two-phone duels (Bluetooth, peer-to-peer Wi-Fi, Nearby Interaction)

For a Duel or Tournament the app connects nearby devices directly over Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. Only what the match needs is exchanged: display name, spells cast, health and energy. Nearby Interaction measures the distance between the two phones to keep a safe gap. Nothing is sent to the internet.

Camera and microphone (Spell Cam)

The camera and microphone are used only when you open Spell Cam to film a duel. Frames are analysed on the device to place spell effects; nothing is uploaded.

Photos

The app only asks for add-only access, to save a clip or picture when you tap Save. It never reads your photo library.

Data on your device

Your settings, stats and the sigil you draw are stored only on your device.

Third parties

No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

Wand Clash collects no personal data from anyone, including children, and has no online chat.

Deleting data

All data lives on your device; deleting the app deletes it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with its date.
