Privacy Policy — ClueLab

Updated: 21 October 2026

ClueLab (Bến Sương Detectives) is a detective puzzle game by Thinkling Studio. This page explains what the app does, and does not do, with your information.

Summary

  • We collect nothing. No personal data, no analytics, no automatic crash reports, no advertising identifiers.
  • No account, no internet, no server. Every case is bundled in the app.
  • No ads, no tracking. “Season 1” is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple Account.

Motion sensors

Some puzzles let you tilt the phone (for example to catch a reflection). Motion data is processed on the device, never stored and never sent. Every such puzzle has a tap alternative.

Microphone (optional)

One puzzle lets you blow gently on the phone. The app asks for the microphone only if you choose that option; sound is used only to detect the breath in that moment, never recorded, stored or sent. You can always tap instead.

Data on your device

Your case progress, rewards and Detective Corner are stored only on your device.

Third parties

No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Children's privacy

ClueLab collects no personal data from anyone, including children, and has no online chat.

Deleting data

All data lives on your device; deleting the app deletes it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with its date.

Contact
